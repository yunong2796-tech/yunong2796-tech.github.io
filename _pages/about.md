I am a Ph.D. student in the department of Computer Science and Engineering from the [University at Buffalo](https://engineering.buffalo.edu/computer-science-engineering.html), advised by Dr. [Haipeng Cai](https://chapering.github.io/). Before that, I was a Ph.D. candidate in the School of Electrical Engineering and Computer Science at [Washington State University](https://school.eecs.wsu.edu/).

My research lies at the intersection of Software Engineering, Program Analysis, and Bug Detection. I develop ML and DL-based techniques for software defect detection, classification, repair.
