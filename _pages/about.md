I am a Ph.D. student in the department Computer Science and Engineering from the [University at Buffalo](https://engineering.buffalo.edu/computer-science-engineering.html), advised by Dr. [Haipeng Cai](https://chapering.github.io/). Before that, I pursued graduate study in Computer Science at [Washington State University](https://school.eecs.wsu.edu/). I received my bachelor's degree in Automation from South China University of Technology.

My research broadly lies in Software Engineering, with interests in program analysis, software testing, bug detection and repair, software reliability, and software data quality. I am interested in empirical and data-driven approaches for understanding, evaluating, and improving complex software systems.
